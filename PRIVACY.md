# Rasid — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Rasid is an order and invoice app for pharmacy distribution, made by Rasid (Lahore, Pakistan). This policy explains what the app does with information.

## Your business data stays on your phone
Your customers, items, prices, orders, invoices, estimates and payments are stored **on your own phone**. We do not upload them to our servers and we cannot see them.

## Google Drive backup (optional)
If you choose **Connect Google Drive**, Rasid asks Google for permission to create and read **only the files Rasid itself makes** (Google's `drive.file` permission). Rasid saves your backup file in a folder called **Rasid Backups** in **your own** Google Drive, so you can restore it on a new phone. Rasid cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive. The backup is sent directly from your phone to your Google Drive — never to us. You can disconnect at any time in **More › Backup & restore**, and you can delete the backups from your Drive yourself.

## Account and activation
When you create your account we receive your **name, email address and mobile number** and your phone's **device ID**, so we can verify your email, activate your licence, keep one account per phone and give you support. We use them only for that and never sell or share them.

## Fingerprint unlock
Fingerprint unlock is handled by Android. Rasid never receives or stores your fingerprint — Android only tells Rasid whether it matched.

## Notifications
Rasid uses notifications for app updates and the optional evening summary. Update alerts are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; no business data is sent with them.

## Support messages
If you send a message from **Contact support**, we receive the message and your account details so we can reply.

## Your choices
You can export, back up, restore or delete your data from inside the app at any time. To ask about your account information or have it removed, email **rasidapppk@gmail.com**.
