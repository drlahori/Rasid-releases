# Rasid — Billing made easy

**Rasid** is an Android app for pharmacy distribution in Pakistan: take orders, make invoices and estimates, track payments and outstanding balances, send orders to your supplier, and back up safely to your own Google Drive.

- Orders, invoices & estimates with PDF sharing
- Customer prices, payments and outstanding balances
- Batch, expiry and stock alerts
- Reports: sales, calendar, best sellers and purchases
- Automatic backup to your own Google Drive
